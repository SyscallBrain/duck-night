Duck Night is an offline reflex game for Android about photographing ducks at dusk.

Take the quick aim-and-react challenge of the old arcade bird games and turn it into photography. There is no hunting here: you frame the bird, press the shutter, and Farrusco, a Portuguese pointer, brings the developed photo back.

CAMPAIGN
- 600 levels across twelve places, 50 per place, each in Easy, Medium and Hard.
- Eight level types: classic, double, flock, short roll, against the clock, protected birds, clays and bosses.
- Twelve legendary birds, each with its own trick and a three-photo album instead of a health bar.
- Wind, fog, snow, rain, night, cover, diving birds, protected birds and the golden duck.

PHOTOGRAPHY
- Tap the bird, move the phone, or aim with your thumb and press the shutter.
- Every photo has a quality from 0 to 100, based on centering, sharpness and wing pose.
- The best photo of each species is kept in the bird book and redrawn from its data.
- Farrusco brings the developed photo back and laughs when a bird escapes.

PLACES
- Village Pond, Wheat Field, Windy Pinewood, Misty Marsh, Stork Meadow, Snowy Ridge, Salt Pans, Autumn Wood, Heath at Night, Storm Delta, Aurora Fjord and the Great Duck's Nest, each with its own painted scene.
- Protected birds must not be photographed: the flash disturbs their nests, and Farrusco shakes his head.

MODES
- Classic arcade with Games A, B and C.
- Daily challenge with a 270-day history.
- Free clay shooting and the village-fair gallery, with a cork rifle.
- Training, and a two-player duel on one device with a privacy curtain.
- Failure replay: the last three seconds, the bird trail and the exact viewfinder of every photo.

PROGRESSION
- Stars for reach and quality, photographer titles, bird book, boss gallery, 45 achievements and statistics.
- Three dogs, decorative cameras and viewfinders.
- No lives, no currency and nothing to buy.

ACCESSIBILITY
- Bigger birds, high-contrast outlines, color-blind palettes and reduced motion.
- Larger text, left-handed play, adjustable game speed and aim assist on Easy.
- Progress export and import.
- English (United States) and European Portuguese.

PRIVACY
- No internet permission. No ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.
- The only permission is vibration, for the shutter and photos.
- Your progress stays on the device, and you can export and import it yourself.
