## Icon

The adaptive icon is drawn as vectors in the repository: `drawable/ic_launcher_foreground.xml` (the dusk sun and an original flying-duck silhouette), `drawable/ic_launcher_monochrome.xml` and the layers in `mipmap-anydpi-v26` and `mipmap-anydpi-v33`. The 512 × 512 store icon and the feature graphic in `fastlane/` are rendered from the same design.

## Scenes, birds, dogs and HUD

Everything is drawn on a Compose canvas or as code vectors; the APK contains no raster art. Interface colors live in the tokens of `ui/theme/DuckTheme.kt`; the colors of the twelve scenes live in the scene recipes of `ui/screens/game/SceneArt.kt`, and the bird colors in `ui/art/BirdArt.kt`.
